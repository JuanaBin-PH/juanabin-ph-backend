# 01 — Executive Summary

JuanaBin PH is a blockchain-powered waste segregation and reward platform built natively on the Stellar network. Launched from Metro Manila, Philippines, JuanaBin PH addresses three interlocking challenges facing Philippine communities in 2026: widespread household waste mismanagement, the financial exclusion of 51 million unbanked Filipinos, and the absence of transparent, tamper-proof waste-diversion records for local government units (LGUs).

This Stellar Community Fund (SCF) Instawards submission funds JuanaBin PH's first production-grade 30-day sprint, running **August 31 – September 29, 2026**. The $5,000 Instaward delivers four verifiable technical deliverables that form the core infrastructure of the platform: a Stellar wallet provisioning system with JBIN custom asset, a Segregate-to-Earn logic engine, an admin dashboard with a public fail-closed verifier, and a reproducibility and evidence package.

## Why JuanaBin PH Qualifies for SCF Instawards (August 2026)

> The Philippines generates approximately **15 million tonnes** of solid waste per year, of which roughly **9 million tonnes** are mismanaged — dumped, burned, or left uncollected. <!-- TODO: cite sources for the 15M and 9M tonne figures --> In 2026, with the Philippine Extended Producer Responsibility Act (RA 11898) in full enforcement and DENR escalating compliance mandates, JuanaBin PH's on-chain waste diversion records are intended to provide the kind of transparent, auditable payout trail that barangay officials, DILG inspectors and carbon market participants can independently verify. ## Instawards Strategic Intent

JuanaBin PH is at the proof-of-concept to MVP transition stage. The Instawards format — short timeline, clear deliverables, verifiable evidence — matches this stage perfectly. The August 31 – September 29, 2026 sprint will demonstrate on-chain functionality with real transactions and a working demo, before requesting larger SCF funding rounds. The four deliverables produced in this sprint form the technical foundation for a full SCF grant application.
