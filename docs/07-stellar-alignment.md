## PASTE-READY BLOCK — STELLAR / SCF ALIGNMENT (SCF SUBMISSION AUGUST 30, 2026)

**Stellar Mission:** "Create equitable access to the global financial system."

**1. Financial Inclusion (Core Stellar Value)**

51M Filipinos are unbanked in 2026. <!-- TODO: cite source for the 51M figure --> JuanaBin PH households receive their first-ever blockchain wallet simply by segregating waste. No bank account, no government ID, no e-wallet needed — just a Kinde signup → auto-provisioned Stellar wallet. First on-chain income for nanays and waste workers.

**2. Real-World Utility, Not Speculation (SCF Priority 2026)**

SCF rejects meme coins. JBIN is not tradeable for profit. 1 JBIN = 1 point, awarded per correctly segregated item, redeemable for real Buslo artisan products (woven since 2016). Fixed utility value, no exchange listing, no fiat off-ramp in this sprint. 100% utility.

**3. Low-Cost Payments at Scale**

Stellar's $0.00001 fee is the ONLY chain where we can pay ₱5 reward per segregation sustainably. Ethereum and Solana would consume 50% of rewards in fees. Stellar makes micro-rewards viable for Philippine barangays. Peso values are indicative for participants; nothing is pegged on-chain.

**4. Transparency for LGUs**

Every JBIN payout is publicly auditable on Stellar Explorer, so the record of which wallet was paid, how much, and when cannot be altered after the fact. This is intended to give barangay officials and independent auditors a verifiable payout trail, and to produce records suitable for RA 9003 and RA 11898 (EPR Act) reporting. The ledger evidences what the system recorded and settled; it does not by itself verify that a segregation event occurred as declared — see [14 §14.2](14-data-and-authorization-policy.md#142-what-the-event-hash-proves).

**5. Circular Economy + Climate**

Less landfill methane, more upcycling via the Buslo ng Pag-asa artisan network. Waste → Wallet → Product. Each reward transaction is a permanent record of a diversion event; CO²e avoided is estimated off-chain from published per-material factors and reported as a project estimate rather than a measurement.

**6. Built on Stellar, Not Just Using Stellar**

We use Stellar Python SDK + Testnet in this sprint (August 31 – September 29, 2026), with JBIN as a classic custom asset — see [13 §13.1](13-tech-stack-and-deployment.md#131-why-a-classic-asset-and-not-a-soroban-contract) for why that primitive fits the scope. Soroban smart contracts for auto-verification are a post-sprint consideration. Kinde auth layer + Stellar = a replicable model for other Philippine impact projects. Open-source, MIT licensed.
