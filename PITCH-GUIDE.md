# JuanaBin PH — Presentation Pitch Guide

<!-- Internal presentation material. This file is NOT part of the SCF Instawards sprint scope and is not a deliverable. It is not linked from the documentation set in docs/. See README.md for the technical scope. -->

Cleaned and organized from the team's notes. Use as the pitch checklist.

## 1. Delivery & Voice

Energy, tone, and enunciation should be harmonious — like a song, never monotone.

Control your dynamics: know when to raise your voice (for emphasis) and when to lower it (to regain composure and pull attention from the judges and audience).

This one simple factor strongly affects the whole presentation.

## 2. Opening Hook

Open with a relatable "Did you know?" question so the audience connects right away (as Sharper Robotics did).

## 3. Pitch Flow — Content

Must fit within the limited time. Deliver in this order:

| # | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Current problem** — state it clearly |
| 2 | **Our solution** — inject JuanaBin PH as the answer |
| 3 | **Target market** — who we serve |
| 4 | **Data & traction** — use actual, verifiable, researched data |
| 5 | **Peso–dollar value** — the economic value |
| 6 | **Pricing** — explain the cost, how you arrived at the SRP, and why it's valuable |
| 7 | **Community / LGU partners** |
| 8 | **Team profiles** — who's on the team |
| 9 | **Roadmap** — where we're headed |
| 10 | **The ask** — what are we asking from the investor? |
| 11 | **SDG goals** — show global reach and impact |

## 4. Technical Questions to Prepare

- How many kilowatts of energy does one JuanaBin PH model use?
- What are we selling — the service, the software, or the hardware? And how (e.g. installment)?

## 5. Closing — Leave a Memory

End with the tagline so it sticks with the judges and audience:

> 🗑️ Bin of Hope. Sort Right. Shoot Like a Champion.

— JuanaBin PH
